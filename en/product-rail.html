Rolling Stock Parts

Hyun Sung specializes in exterior and structural parts for railway vehicles. Using high-precision press and laser processing, we produce parts of the highest quality.

🚃 SIDE FRAME

Side structural parts for railway vehicles. High-rigidity materials maximize durability.

🔩 Car Body Structural Parts

Complete range of precision parts for the inner structure of railway car bodies.

⚙️ Exterior Panels

High-quality exterior panels made with laser cutting and forming technology.
