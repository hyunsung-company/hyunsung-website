Cyber Reporting Center

To protect the safety and rights of employees, Hyun Sung operates an open communication channel with strictly guaranteed anonymity.
All reports can be submitted anonymously without worrying about being identified, and the content of reports and the reporter’s information are kept strictly confidential.

Types of reports

  • Corruption, bribery, embezzlement, use of insider information
  • Abuse of authority, improper orders, conflicts of interest
  • Fair trade law violations, trade secret infringement
  • Human rights violations, workplace harassment, sexual harassment and assault
  • Safety and health violations, near-miss reports

Protection of reporters

  • Confidentiality: no disclosure or hinting of the reporter’s identity
  • Job security: no disadvantage of any kind for making a report
  • Reduced liability: disciplinary action may be reduced or waived for voluntary reports
