ESG Management

🌿 Environmental

We recognize the role and responsibility of companies in reducing greenhouse gases, and will join global efforts to achieve carbon neutrality.

  • Reducing carbon emissions and responding to the climate crisis
  • Energy efficiency improvement activities
  • Management of environmental pollutants (air, water, waste, etc.)

🤝 Social

We will keep thinking about the right direction for sustainable growth and the right changes for society, and work to spread social value.

  • Worker safety
  • Supply chain management
  • Responsible purchasing and procurement
  • Compliance with labor standards and protection of human rights

⚖️ Governance

We will compete fairly in order to grow responsibly in social, economic and environmental terms.

  • Transparent accounting and responsible tax payment
  • Identifying stakeholders and managing risks
  • Reports from internal and external customers
  • Operating a grievance handling system
