Ethical Management

 

Hyun Sung Co., Ltd. Ethical Management Policy Statement

 

Hyun Sung Co., Ltd. aims to be a company trusted by all stakeholders through transparent and fair management, and adopts ethical management as a core management principle.

 

The company establishes and operates an ethical management policy that includes the following.

 

1. Anti-corruption and prohibition of improper solicitation

  • No giving or receiving of money, gifts, entertainment or bribes
  • Compliance with domestic and international anti-corruption laws
 

2. Prevention of conflicts of interest

  • Preventing conflicts between personal interests and the interests of the company
  • Advance reporting and management of any conflict of interest that arises
 

3. Prevention of anti-competitive conduct and unfair trade

  • No collusion, abuse of bargaining position or unfair competition
  • Maintaining fair and transparent contracting and trading practices
 

4. Prevention of counterfeit parts

  • No purchase, use or distribution of counterfeit or uncertified parts
  • Managing the origin and history of parts in the supply chain
 

5. Compliance with export restrictions and trade regulations

  • Compliance with import/export control laws and sanctions regulations
  • Following prior approval procedures for controlled items
 

6. Information protection

  • Protecting confidential and personal information of customers and the company
  • Preventing misuse and leakage of internal information
 

7. Protection of intellectual property rights

  • Respecting the intellectual property rights of others
  • Protecting the company's technology, designs and trade secrets
 

This policy is the highest-level principle above the Ethics Charter, Code of Ethics and Ethics Practice Guidelines, and applies to all employees and stakeholders.
