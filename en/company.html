Message from the CEO

“We will grow together with our customers and become a company they can trust.”

Hello.
Thank you for visiting our website.

Since our founding in 1988, we have grown as a specialized manufacturer of railway vehicle and defense parts, steel-mill equipment, shipbuilding parts and industrial machinery, achieving customer satisfaction through steady technology development and quality improvement.

Drawing on our field experience and technical expertise, we do our best to produce products our customers can trust, and we regard quality, on-time delivery and safety as the fundamental values of our company.

Based on ESG management, we also practice environmental protection, industrial safety, ethical management and win-win cooperation, striving to develop as a sustainable company. Through change and innovation, we will become a company that satisfies our customers even more.

All of our employees will always strive to provide better quality and service, based on a sense of responsibility and sincerity.
We ask for your continued interest and support as we grow together with our customers and become a company they can trust.
Thank you.

Hyun Sung Co., Ltd.  CEO Choi Seok-rim
